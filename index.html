About

I don't write code.
I architect systems.

I'm Luca Lo Cricchio — a Full Stack Developer from Monza, Italy, born in 2007. While most people my age were still figuring out HTML, I was already building real digital systems. I didn't wait for permission. I started doing.

My approach is that of a digital architect: I think in full systems before writing a single line. From front-end to back-end, from relational databases to advertising campaigns, from automation to the creative direction of a game studio — every piece has to fit perfectly.

I've already worked with real clients, managed campaigns reaching thousands of impressions, and co-founded a video game studio. This isn't ambition — it's already history.

Origin Story

How a developer
is forged

2021

The beginning: ITIS Ettore Molinari

At 14 I enrolled at ITIS Ettore Molinari in Milan to study Computer Science. I immediately understood that school is a launchpad, not a ceiling. Every concept learned in class became a brick for building something real outside.

2023

LSD Software Studio: building worlds

In November 2023 I co-founded LSD Software — a video game studio. I became lead developer and creative director. Not theory: real production. I shipped Card Game, a casino-themed guess game, and P-E-S-C-A, an original point & click adventure.

Learning to manage a creative project, coordinate a vision, and turn an idea into a product people can actually play — that was my first real lesson in leadership.

2024

The real world: internship at EUDEMONIA SRL

June 2024. First real internship, first company, first real client. At EUDEMONIA SRL I managed campaigns on Google Ads, Meta Business Suite and Amazon Business. I analysed traffic data, optimised performance, and built idraulicoservizi.com from scratch with WordPress and SEO.

I discovered that code is only half the game. The other half is understanding what the client wants, what their audience searches for, and how to translate data into decisions.

2024–25

Certifications, expansion, new horizons

While others sleep, I study. Oracle Academy, Cisco, Sorint.Lab: database design, networking, Python, React Native, Docker, AWS, web security, AI for developers. Every certification isn't a badge — it's another tool in the kit.

I shipped LSD Console, a 2D home-console-style title, and started Project Arcana, a psychological 3D story-driven game. The technical complexity rises — and so do I.

2025

Back in the field: impresaedileabilart.com

Second internship at EUDEMONIA. This time I managed everything independently: site structure, SEO content, advertising campaigns and direct client contact. impresaedileabilart.com went live — a complete digital system conceived and built by me.

2026

Diploma and next chapter

July 2026: Computer Science diploma from ITIS Ettore Molinari. The diploma is a starting point, not a finish line. I already have skills that most people chase for years, real-world experience, and a portfolio that speaks for itself.

The next chapter is being written right now. And it will be the most interesting one yet.
